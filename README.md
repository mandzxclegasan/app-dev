1. BOOK REVIEW: PROJECT HAIL MARY

Author: Andy Weir
Genre: Science Fiction / Space Opera
Year Published: 2021
Page Count: 496 pages

Overview & Analysis
Ryland Grace wakes up from a long coma aboard a spacecraft with total amnesia, millions of miles from Earth alongside two deceased crewmates. As his memory returns, he discovers he is the sole survivor of a desperate mission to save Earth's sun from an extinction-level threat. The novel stands out for its reliance on real-world physics, biology, and trial-and-error problem-solving, paired with an unexpected and heartwarming interstellar friendship.

2. MOVIE REVIEW: PROJECT HAIL MARY (FILM ADAPTATION)

Directors: Phil Lord and Christopher Miller
Starring: Ryan Gosling, Sandra Hüller
Genre: Science Fiction / Adventure
Release Year: 2026

Overview & Analysis
Bringing Andy Weir’s bestselling novel to the big screen, this adaptation translates the high-stakes tension and humor of the book into a visually striking space adventure. Ryan Gosling anchors the film with a balance of vulnerability and wit as Ryland Grace, while Greig Fraser’s cinematography captures the vast isolation of deep space. It successfully preserves the scientific curiosity and emotional core of the source material.

3. SERIES REVIEW: SILO

Creator: Graham Yost (Based on the novels by Hugh Howey)
Starring: Rebecca Ferguson, Tim Robbins, Common
Genre: Dystopian Science Fiction / Mystery
Platform: Apple TV+

Overview & Analysis
Set in a ruined, toxic future, Silo follows a community of thousands living in a giant underground bunker extending hundreds of stories deep. Citizens live under strict regulations meant to protect them from the outside world, yet anyone who asks to go outside is never seen alive again. Driven by strong performances—especially Rebecca Ferguson as engineer Juliette Nichols—the series excels at slow-burn world-building, political intrigue, and uncovering buried truths.
